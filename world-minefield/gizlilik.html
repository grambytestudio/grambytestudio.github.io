Privacy Policy

World Minefield, GramByte Studio. Last updated: 28 November 2026

Your progress and settings stay on your device; the app has no account. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which may process data as described in section 2.

1. What is stored, and where

Your progress (cleared levels, best times, clean streaks and the rules you have seen) and your settings (sound, vibration, hold time and language) are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are used to continue your game and remember your choices.

The app does not ask for your name, e-mail address or any other personal information, and it does not send your game data to us or to a server of ours. This does not mean the ad service makes no network connections (see section 2).

2. Ads

The app is free and supported by Google AdMob ads. A full-screen ad may appear after you finish a level, won or lost, at most once every 90 seconds; no ad comes on its own while you play. You can also choose to watch an ad to get a hint, or to continue once after hitting a mine; those ads play only when you ask.

Where the law asks for consent, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and ads are requested only when the form allows it. There you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your IP address (which can also be used to estimate your approximate location), device identifiers such as the advertising ID, ad and app interactions, and performance and diagnostic information, for ad delivery, measurement and the prevention of abuse, and may share them with third-party ad partners. If your device asks whether the app may track you across apps, you can decline; ads are then shown without cross-app tracking data. You can manage your advertising ID and ad privacy options in your device's privacy or ads settings; the options depend on the device and its version.

3. Network and permissions

The game itself works offline. Ads need an internet connection, so on Android the app has the internet permission; without a connection there are simply no ads. The app asks for no other permission: no location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or notifications. It uses no separate analytics or crash reporting service; the measurement and diagnostics of the ad SDK are described above.

4. Map and place data

The world map, the coastlines and the city names come from Natural Earth, which is in the public domain. They are packaged inside the app. The map shows no country borders.

5. Service providers and security

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may process data on servers outside your country, and its services use encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Google's privacy information: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. Backups

If your device's built-in backup is turned on, the operating system may include the app's data in the backup of the account you are signed in with, and restore it on a new device. This is done by the operating system, not by the app, and you can turn it off in your device settings.

7. Deleting your data

Settings > Reset progress erases your progress. Clearing the app's data in your device settings, or uninstalling the app, erases everything the app stores. For ad data, use the choices in section 2 and Google's privacy tools.

8. Your rights

Under data protection laws such as the GDPR and the Turkish KVKK you have the right to know whether your data is processed, to ask about it, and to have it corrected or deleted, and to object. Your game data is only on your device, so you can use most of these rights in the app itself; for ad data use the options in section 2 and Google's tools, and write to us with any question.

9. Children

The app does not ask anyone, children included, for personal information; the data the ad service processes is described in section 2. Use of the app by minors is subject to the applicable age rules and, where required, the consent of a parent or guardian.

10. Changes

If the app's features or the services it uses change, this page is updated and the date above changes.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, write to GramByte Studio at grambytestudio@gmail.com.
